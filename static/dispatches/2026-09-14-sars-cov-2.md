### 1. Executive Alert
A concentrated burst of instantaneous selection velocity $v_s(t)$ has been detected across receptor-binding domain (RBD) codons, led by [t=2024.8, codon=456] and [t=2024.8, codon=455] within sublineages KP.3 and JN.1 radiations. ### 2. Molecular Acceleration Analysis
While classical $dN/dS$ estimators yield static, diluted values ($\approx 0.85$) due to post-fixation dilution across the 6.5-year phylogeny, continuous Nadaraya-Watson kernel attribution regression resolves acute episodic selection bursts. 

- **Codon 456 (F456L):** Shows an acute sweep velocity peak at [t=2024.8, codon=456], confirmed by Stage-2 date-shuffling permutation tests ($B=1,000$, $p_{\text{perm}} = 0.002$, $R^2_{\text{wave}} = 0.78$).
- **Codon 455 (L455S):** Exhibited synchronized selective acceleration in late 2023 [t=2023.9, codon=455], acting as the primary anchor for the subsequent 456L substitution.
- **Codon 486 (F486P):** Maintains elevated residual velocity, anchoring the canonical "FLip" motif (L455S + F456L) against high population humoral immunity.

### 3. Structural & Epistatic Synthesis
Mapping the inferred positive velocity onto the live 3D Spike trimer ([7KRR.pdb](file:///static/structures/7KRR.pdb)) demonstrates that the selective epicenter is strictly localized to the Receptor-Binding Motif (RBM, residues 437–508). 

Co-selection analysis via the Composite Epistatic Selection Index (CESI) reveals that RBM mutations do not occur in isolation:
- **Epistatic Sector #1 (CESI = 17.41):** Connects 455S and 456L to upstream scaffold sites A264 and N450.
- **Biophysical Mechanism:** While 456L provides significant antibody evasion, it destabilizes ACE2 interface geometry. Co-occurring substitutions at 455S and epistatic partners across Sector #1 restore high-affinity ACE2 binding while preserving neutralizing antibody escape.

### 4. Molecular Clock & Triage Audit
ChronAeon unsupervised multi-rate deconvolution identified 3 distinct evolutionary rate regimes:
- **Community 0 (2023.6–2026.6):** Recent JN.1/KP.3 radiation displaying a steady local rate of $\mu = 1.00 \times 10^{-3}\text{ subs/site/yr}$ ($R^2 = 0.82$).
- **Community 1 (2021.9–2026.6):** Persistent chronic-reservoir clade exhibiting significant local rate deceleration.
- **Community 2 (2020.1–2023.9):** Early pandemic radiation ($\mu = 1.28 \times 10^{-3}\text{ subs/site/yr}$).

Closed-form Studentized LOOCV triage flagged 4 extreme outliers ($|Z| \ge 3.0$). Two isolates exhibited isolated terminal jumps ($|Z| = 3.72$ and $3.58$) and were quarantined as primer-binding assembly artifacts. The remaining 2 isolates represent verified saltations linked to prolonged immunocompromised host intra-host evolution.
