### 1. Executive Summary & Surveillance Status
Continuous positive selection velocity $v_s(t)$ computed across the 6.5-year longitudinal alignment of SARS-CoV-2 Spike reveals that **no acute positive sweep velocity surges are currently active** in the contemporary surveillance horizon ($t = 2026.65$). The historical peak velocity events documented in the dataset—most notably at [t=2023.8, codon=455] ($v_s = 0.0412\text{ subs/site/yr}$, marking the emergence of JN.1) and [t=2024.8, codon=456] ($v_s = 0.0057\text{ subs/site/yr}$, the KP.3 "FLip" radiation)—occurred 2 to 3 years ago and have since achieved fixation or selective exhaustion. Current instantaneous selection velocities across all Spike codons have cooled to baseline levels ($\max v_s(t) < 0.005\text{ subs/site/yr}$). Surveillance status is classified as **Nominal**.

### 2. Molecular Acceleration Analysis (HyphAeon Temporal)
Velocity values are inferred using **HyphAeon temporal surveillance** (`hyphaeon temporal`), which evaluates positive selection along a continuous timeline via Nadaraya-Watson kernel regression:
$$v_s(t) = \max\left(0, \frac{d}{dt}\hat{a}_s(t)\right)$$
where $\hat{a}_s(t)$ represents the continuous non-synonymous selective intensity. Unlike classical static $dN/dS$ (which yields diluted values $\approx 0.85$ over multi-year phylogenies), continuous temporal modeling captures the precise rise and fall of selective bursts:

- **Codon 455 (L455S / "FLip" Anchor):** Exhibited its primary adaptive acceleration in late 2023 [t=2023.8, codon=455] ($v_s = 0.0412\text{ subs/site/yr}$, $p_{\text{perm}} = 0.032$). Contemporary velocity at $t = 2026.65$ has decayed to $< 0.0001\text{ subs/site/yr}$, reflecting post-sweep fixation.
- **Codon 456 (F456L):** Experienced peak episodic acceleration in 2024 [t=2024.8, codon=456] during the KP.3 expansion, subsequently plateauing into fixation.
- **Contemporary Horizon (2026.65):** The maximum positive velocity currently observed across all 1,273 Spike residues is baseline drift ($v_s \le 0.0046\text{ subs/site/yr}$ at codon 839), with zero sites exceeding emergency surveillance thresholds ($\ge 0.010$).

- **Epistatic Sector #1 (CESI = 17.41):** Pairwise co-selection analysis via the Composite Epistatic Selection Index (CESI) reveals that the historical RBM sweeps (L455S, F456L) strongly coupled with upstream NTD scaffold sites A264 and N450.
- **Biophysical Mechanism:** The primary immune escape substitution (456L) incurred an energetic penalty at the ACE2 interface. Co-selected substitutions at 455S and epistatic partners across Sector #1 restored high-affinity ACE2 binding while preserving neutralizing antibody escape. In the current epoch, Sector #1 is fixed across circulating lineages.
